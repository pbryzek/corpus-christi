Our Mission
We're A Parish Shaped by A Community Who Loves It.
For nearly 100 years, Corpus Christi has been shaped by the people who show up — who serve, who welcome the stranger, and who keep returning because something here is real.
"One of the greatest joys of my life is telling others about Corpus Christi. And the fact that I call it a family tells you just about everything you need to know. We're not a perfect community — we're a real one. Big hopes, full hearts, and a hunger for something meaningful. And somehow, every Sunday, something happens. Strangers become neighbors. Neighbors become friends. Friends become family. Some people walk in a little weary and walk out lighter. It always moves me when I see and feel it. Everyone belongs here — all we ask is that you give us a chance to prove it."
— Fr. Leo, Pastor
→ Optional: pair with Fr. Leo's welcome video thumbnail
Fr. Leo means every word of that. And he'll be the first to tell you: he doesn't define this parish. You do.
Your joy.Your challenges.Your openness.Your gifts.Your contributions.
That's what makes Corpus Christi alive.

Why Corpus?
Some Things You Just Have to Experience.
The music. The welcome. The school. Father Leo... and a community that shows up for each other in ways that still surprise us.

But don't take our word for it.

We continue to evolve — and we're 100 years young. Some things, though, have never changed:
We take faith seriously — and ourselves lightly.
Expect substance, humor, and homilies worth talking about at brunch.
Our community found us as much as we found them.
Across generations, backgrounds, and life stories — the diversity here just happened naturally. And it's one of our greatest gifts.
We wrestle with faith openly.
We're a community that sits with complexity, not one that pretends it's simple.
We don't just welcome you — we accept you.
Not despite who you are, but because of who you are.

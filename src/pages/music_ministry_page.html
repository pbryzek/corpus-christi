Where Music Becomes Prayer
Our Music Director, Joseph Hébert, Has Performed with Jon Batiste, Harry Connick Jr., Santana, Stevie Wonder, Ray Charles, Placido Domingo, and Leonard Bernstein. He's Won Grammys and Performed at the Vatican.
And at every Mass, he directs our incredible choir.
Joe could be anywhere, and he's here. And in true Corpus Christi fashion, he wants to work with anyone who loves music — no experience required.

Come hear what Sunday sounds like. And if you sing or play — keep reading.

Section 3 — Join the Choir
There's a Place for Every Voice.
Our choirs are one of the most beautiful expressions of who we are — a community where everyone truly belongs. Whether you've sung your whole life or never set foot in a choir, there's a place for your voice here. Choir here isn't about perfection — we trust the Holy Spirit to bring the harmony. And every singer who joins has access to Joe's mentorship, at no cost. Wow.

Joe Hébert
Music Director — Corpus Christi Parish since 1988
ccmusic322@gmail.com
Full biography, collaborator list & film credits → josephhebert.com
The most inspiring part of his ministry
"I'm deeply moved by the courage it takes to sing through fear. When we trust our voices and say yes to the moment, we make room for God to work miracles, big and small."
Saint he'd have coffee with
"Mother Teresa of Calcutta. I'd ask her how we move beyond our fears to trust in the love and mercy of God. And I'd share her two sayings that changed my life: 'A smile is the beginning of love.' And: 'We cannot all do great things. But we can do small things with great love.'"
Song that feels like God
"Challenging question for a songwriter and musician. I'll start with the classical piece Nimrod by Edward Elgar."
Prayer life in three words
"Vulnerability, Gratitude, Trust."
Street Cred
Classically trained musician. Symphony, orchestral conducting, choral directing, studio recordings, and performance tours throughout the US and beyond. BA Music Performance, Cal State Hayward. Graduate Studies in Pastoral Ministry, Holy Names University. Post-Baccalaureate Studies in Cello Performance, Stanford University. Director of Music Ministry in the Diocese of Oakland since 1988. "I've spent so long in the Diocese, I'm now 60% holy water and 40% sheet music."
After Mass ritual
"Starbucks — a comfortable atmosphere where I can count on parishioners coming through for a quick hello or cup of coffee."
